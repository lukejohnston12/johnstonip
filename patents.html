Choose the right kind of patent

A patent lets you stop others from making, using, selling or importing your invention. Which kind you need depends on what makes your idea valuable, and many products deserve more than one.

TypeWhat it protectsTerm
Utility patentHow something works: processes, machines, manufactured articles and compositions of matter.20 yrs from filing
Design patentHow something looks: the new, original ornamental design of a product.15 yrs from grant
Provisional applicationAn early filing date and “patent pending” status while you refine, test or raise money.12-month bridge

Utility patents require maintenance fees to stay in force. Terms can be adjusted in some cases.

Before you show it, sell it or pitch it

In the U.S., your own public disclosure generally starts a one-year clock to file. Many other countries allow no grace period at all. If you’ve already disclosed, call us now.
