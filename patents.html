The right patent for what you’ve made

A patent gives you the right to stop others from making, using, selling or importing your invention. Which kind you need depends on what makes your idea valuable.

Utility patent

How something works: new processes, machines, articles of manufacture and compositions of matter.

20 years from filing

Design patent

How something looks: the new, original ornamental design of a product.

15 years from grant

Provisional application

An early filing date and “patent pending” status while you refine, test or fundraise.

How we work

1. Patentability search

Before you invest in an application, we look at what’s already out there and tell you candidly whether — and how — your invention can be protected.

2. Drafting that holds up

A patent is only as strong as its claims. With a mechanical engineering background, Bob Johnston works through the technical details with you to claim your invention broadly and precisely.

3. Prosecution at the USPTO

We handle the back-and-forth with the patent examiner — responses, amendments and interviews — and keep you informed in plain English at each step.

4. Portfolio strategy

Continuations, design patents alongside utility patents, international filings and freedom-to-operate questions — we help your portfolio support your business goals and your next raise.

Before you show it, sell it or pitch it — call us. In the U.S., your own public disclosure starts a one-year clock to file. In many other countries, disclosing before filing can forfeit protection entirely.
