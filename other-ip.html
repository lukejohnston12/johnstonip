Copyright registrations

Copyright protects original creative work — software, writing, photography, art, music and video. Registration with the U.S. Copyright Office is generally required before you can sue over a U.S. work, and registering promptly can unlock statutory damages and attorney’s fees.

Trade secret programs

Formulas, methods, customer lists and know-how can be protected indefinitely — as long as you take reasonable steps to keep them secret. We help you put the right agreements, policies and habits in place.
