Copyright registration

Copyright protects original creative work: software, writing, photography, art, music and video. It exists the moment the work is created, but registration with the U.S. Copyright Office is generally required before you can sue over a U.S. work, and registering promptly can make statutory damages and attorney’s fees available.

We register key works, untangle who owns what when freelancers and agencies are involved, and respond when your content is copied.

Trade secrets

Formulas, methods, source code, customer lists and know-how can be protected indefinitely, as long as you take reasonable steps to keep them secret. Unlike a patent, nothing is published.

We help you decide what to patent and what to keep secret, then put the agreements, policies and habits in place that keep secrets protectable.

Licensing & agreements

IP only creates value when ownership is clear. We draft and negotiate license agreements, assignments, NDAs and the IP terms in employee, contractor and joint development agreements.

If you’re raising money or preparing to sell, we’ll make sure the chain of title holds up in due diligence.

Disputes & enforcement

When someone copies your brand, product or content, or claims you copied theirs, a measured first move matters. We assess your position, send or answer cease-and-desist letters, handle USPTO oppositions and support litigation counsel.
