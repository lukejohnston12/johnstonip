Our founder

Robert “Bob” Johnston III

Bob founded Johnston IP Law, PLLC to help companies and individuals build value and protect their future through trademarks and patents.

Before starting the firm, he practiced at multinational law firms and served as chief intellectual property counsel for a technology startup that achieved a market capitalization of more than a billion dollars. That in-house seat taught him what IP is actually for: supporting a business as it launches, raises money and grows.

Trained as a mechanical engineer, Bob prepares and prosecutes trademark and patent applications before the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office. His clients range from solo founders to established companies in medical devices, software, energy, hospitality, retail and aviation. His experience includes a two-week patent jury trial with a favorable verdict and a patent reexamination involving a medical device.

He has been named a D Magazine Best Lawyer in multiple years and has served as chair of the Dallas Bar Association’s Intellectual Property Section.
