Our founder

Robert “Bob” Johnston III

Bob Johnston founded Johnston IP Law, PLLC to help companies and individuals build value and protect their future through trademarks and patents. Before starting the firm, he practiced at multinational law firms and served as chief intellectual property counsel for a technology startup that achieved a market capitalization of over a billion dollars.

Trained as a mechanical engineer, Bob prepares and prosecutes trademark and patent applications before the United States Patent and Trademark Office. He has helped clients across the full spectrum of intellectual property, in industries from medical devices, software and energy to hospitality, retail and aviation. His experience includes a two-week patent jury trial with a favorable verdict and a patent reexamination involving a medical device.

Bob has been named a D Magazine Best Lawyer in multiple years and has served as chair of the Dallas Bar Association’s Intellectual Property Section.
