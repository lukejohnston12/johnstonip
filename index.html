What getting to ® looks like.

Most of the work that decides whether a trademark registers happens before the USPTO ever sees the application. Here’s the path, and where we come in.

  1. To startDiscovery session

    A free conversation about your business and your brand.

  2. Before you commitClearance

    We search for conflicts and tell you candidly how risky the name is.

  3. Day one at the USPTOApplication

    Goods and services described precisely, on the right filing basis.

  4. Months laterExamination

    If the examiner objects, we answer with argument and evidence.

  5. 30-day windowPublication

    Third parties can oppose. We defend your application if they do.

  6. The goalRegistered

    Nationwide rights, the ® symbol, and a calendar for renewals.
