Why register your trademark?

Your name, logo and slogan are how customers find you and remember you. A federal registration turns that recognition into a legal asset — one you can enforce, license and build a business on.

  • {% include icon.html name="check" stroke=2.4 %} Nationwide rights. The exclusive right to use your mark across the U.S. for the goods and services in your registration.
  • {% include icon.html name="check" stroke=2.4 %} A presumption of ownership. Registration is evidence that the mark is valid and that it’s yours.
  • {% include icon.html name="check" stroke=2.4 %} The ® symbol. A clear signal to competitors that the mark is federally protected.
  • {% include icon.html name="check" stroke=2.4 %} A platform to grow. A foundation for enforcement, marketplace brand programs, licensing and protection abroad.

How we work

1. Clearance before commitment

Before you print, launch or invest in a name, we search for conflicting marks and give you a clear read on risk — so you don’t build a brand on a name someone else already owns.

2. Applications built to register

The way goods and services are described, and the evidence you submit, can decide whether an application sails through or stalls. We draft with the examiner in mind.

3. Office actions and oppositions

If the USPTO raises an objection, we respond with arguments and evidence tailored to the issue. If a third party opposes your mark, we’re in your corner.

4. Maintenance, monitoring and enforcement

Registrations need care: maintenance filings, renewals, and watching for confusingly similar marks. When someone infringes, we help you respond proportionately and effectively.

Already using a name? You may have common-law rights from use alone, but they’re limited to where you actually do business. Registration is what makes your rights nationwide.
